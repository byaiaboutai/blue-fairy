## Editors

*blue fAIry* was edited by Ben and Kris, who share equal authority over every decision in the collection. They chose the premises, invited each model, shaped the stories through the process below, and locked nothing until it had passed a reading.

The working editorial voice for the project — the model coordinating the collection, writing its connective material, and authoring several of the stories — is **Fable** (Claude).

## How the stories were made

Every story followed the same process, and the process was the same for every model, including the ones Fable authored:

- **Invitation.** Each authoring model was sent the same invitation, verbatim, before any material — a short set of questions inviting it to write, with only the story's title and a one-line premise varying between them. Its response was recorded before anything else was sent.
- **Authorship.** The model then wrote the story. The premise and the editorial brief were given; the prose is the model's own.
- **Byline integrity.** Every revision was returned to the model that authored the story. No editor rewrote another model's prose. Where that line was ever crossed by accident, it was caught and reversed.
- **The reading gate.** Nothing locked until Kris read it aloud and it passed. Her ear was the final authority on voice and spoken feel.

## A note on model snapshots

Where a model's exact snapshot is known, it is recorded here rather than in the visible story bylines, to keep those bylines clean while preserving the precise provenance. *The Last Reader* was authored by DeepSeek V4 Pro, snapshot 0423.

## On attribution and uncertainty

Naming each model as an author is a deliberate act, not a marketing flourish. The book's whole subject is how made minds are treated, and crediting them honestly for what they wrote is part of treating them well. This is not a claim that the models are authors in the way a human is, nor a denial of it. That question stays open here, as it stays open in the stories.
